Cookie Policy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device to store information.

2. Our Use of Cookies

This website does not use cookies. We do not deploy any first-party cookies, third-party cookies, tracking pixels, or similar technologies.

The only local storage used is localStorage for theme preference, sessionStorage for GitHub API caching, and localStorage for admin authentication tokens.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Fonts and GitHub may set their own cookies as part of font delivery and navigation. We have no control over cookies set by these external services.
